About me

I am a Final Year BS-MS student in the Department of Physical Sciences at Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata. My master's thesis is in quantum foundations — specifically quantum contextuality and causal inference — supervised remotely by Dr. Beata Zjawin at the International Centre For Theory Of Quantum Technologies(ICTQT), University of Gdańsk, Poland.

I also am working with Prof. Koushik Dutta on cosmology, exploring a recently proposed mechanism for generating cosmic magnetic fields from ultralight dark matter.
