About me

I am a fourth-year undergraduate student in the BS-MS dual degree program at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Kolkata. My academic focus is rooted in theoretical physics.

My primary research interests include early-universe cosmology, inflationary cosmology, high-energy physics, general relativity, and foundational quantum mechanics. I am particularly interested in the concept of time in quantum mechanics and exploring quantum speed limits.

Currently, I am engaging in research projects exploring the fundamental nature of our universe. I view the roles of a teacher and a communicator as fundamentally the same, and I am passionate about making complex physical theories accessible.
