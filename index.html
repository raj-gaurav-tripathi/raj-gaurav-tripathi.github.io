About me

I am a Professor of Computational Sciences at the Institute for Advanced Studies, where I lead the ORION Lab. Our work sits at the intersection of reliable systems, mathematical modelling, and human-centred technology.

My group is interested in how complex tools can remain understandable, useful, and trustworthy. We build methods that help people make better decisions with data, simulation, and intelligent systems.

Before joining the institute, I worked across research and industry teams in Europe and Asia. I enjoy collaborating with students, practitioners, and researchers who care about careful work and open exchange of ideas.

Outside direct research, I contribute to teaching, mentoring, and community projects. I am always happy to hear from prospective students and thoughtful collaborators.
