Lecture Notes

This is a collection of my study notes, derivations, and reviews on various topics in theoretical physics. They are heavily inspired by the clarity and rigor of standard lecture notes in the field. Please feel free to use them, and let me know if you find any typos!

Quantum Speed Limits and Foundations

These notes explore the fundamental physical limits on the evolution of quantum systems. We build from the basic principles of unitary dynamics and energy-time uncertainty up to modern applications in quantum information theory and causal inference.

Available as a PDF

  • 1. Introduction to Quantum Evolution: Unitary dynamics, the Schrödinger equation, and state distinguishability.
  • 2. The Mandelstam-Tamm Bound: Derivations using the Robertson uncertainty relation, energy variance, and orthogonal states.
  • 3. The Margolus-Levitin Bound: Mean energy constraints, geometric interpretations on the Bloch sphere.
  • 4. Contextuality and Causal Inference: Connecting bounds to fundamental quantum contexts.

Early Universe Cosmology

An introduction to the physics of the early universe. These notes cover standard Big Bang cosmology, inflation, and the generation of cosmic magnetic fields from ultralight dark matter, based on recent literature and my work with Prof. Koushik Dutta.

Available as a PDF

  • 1. The Expanding Universe: FRW metric, Friedmann equations, and thermal history.
  • 2. Inflationary Cosmology: The horizon and flatness problems, scalar fields, and slow-roll dynamics.
  • 3. Cosmological Perturbation Theory: Metric and matter perturbations, power spectra.
  • 4. Cosmic Magnetic Fields: Magnetogenesis, ultralight dark matter couplings, and observational constraints.

General Relativity

My foundational notes on General Relativity, focusing heavily on the mathematical machinery of differential geometry and its application to Einstein's field equations.

Available as a PDF

  • 1. Manifolds and Tensors: Vectors, dual vectors, tensors, and the metric.
  • 2. Curvature: Covariant derivatives, Christoffel symbols, Riemann and Ricci tensors.
  • 3. Einstein's Equations: The Einstein-Hilbert action, energy-momentum tensors.
  • 4. Black Holes: The Schwarzschild solution, event horizons, and Kruskal-Szekeres coordinates.
